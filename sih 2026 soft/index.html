For Citizens / Local Residents

  • Pre-Monsoon Check: Inspect retaining walls around house foundations for cracks or tilting. Clear drainage paths of leaves and silt.
  • Observe Warnings: Evacuate immediately when a **Yellow or Orange Alert** changes to a **Red Alert** for your sector.
  • Emergency Kit: Maintain a grab-bag with water, dry rations, first-aid, flashlights, and regional radio receivers.
  • During Heavy Rainfall: Stay alert for sudden muddy discharges, cracking sounds in hills, falling rocks, or trees tilting. These are precursors to slope failure.
  • After the Event: Avoid returning to slide areas immediately. Secondary failures (mudslides or block falls) are highly common.

For District & State Disaster Authorities

  • Trigger Bulletins: Automate warning broadcasts over SMS/Megaphones when rainfall crosses GSI empirical thresholds (e.g., >60mm in 24h).
  • Early Stabilization: Apply geotextiles, shotcrete, or soil-nailing to critical road cuts along highway arteries (NH-10, NH-2) before the monsoon season.
  • Staging Centers: Establish shelter points in flat terrains away from drainage gullies. Ensure satellite phone links are active.
  • GIS Hazard Mapping: Incorporate zoning overlays in municipal building permissions. Ban excavation of hillsides above 35° gradient without engineering clearances.
  • Evacuation Routes: Map alternate rural connectivity routes in case main transport arteries are cut off by debris blocks.
